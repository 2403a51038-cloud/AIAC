Beneath the sky so vast and wide,
The river hums its gentle tide,
Each leaf that sways in whispered cheer
Carries stories for those who hear.

The dawn spills gold on fields anew,
While dreams take flight in morning’s hue,
A single ray can light the way,
Even through clouds of storm and gray.

The heart holds fires that never fade,
Through shadowed paths the soul has made,
And still we walk, with hope our guide,
Toward horizons yet untried.
